# Approved website content

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This record combines the owner redesign brief, subsequent clarifications, the approved wireframe specification, and owner confirmation of operational capability scope.

## Positioning

- Product: self-ordering and connected checkout kiosk software for Philippine businesses.
- Initial emphasis: cafés, bakeries, restaurants, quick-service, and hospitality/food.
- Additional suitable markets: retail, shops, wellness, beauty, services, B2B, and other customer-facing operations.
- Primary theme: Self-Ordering Kiosk Philippines.
- Tagline: Your Menu. Their Order. One Simple Screen.
- Primary CTA: Request a Demo.
- Product-demo CTA: Explore EZ Kyosk, linking to `https://reniel-relearn.github.io/EZ-Kyosk/`.
- Current website language: English.

## Operational core confirmed by the owner

- Self-ordering journey: Browse → Select → Adjust → Review → Confirm.
- Digital menus/catalogs, categories, products, options, quantities, cart, order review, calculated totals, and confirmation.
- Order queueing and tracking.
- POS and checkout connection.
- Payment processing through the approved AltPayNet relationship.
- Approved card, QR, GCash, Maya, and e-wallet payment paths, with deployment requirements confirmed during consultation.
- Real-time inventory.

## Configurable or assessment-dependent scope

- Reports and analytics.
- Customer profiles, loyalty, promotions, bundles, and repeat-order tools.
- Bespoke workflows and other client-requested features.
- Specific POS/provider compatibility.
- Hardware model, supply, ownership, terminal, printer, kitchen, and handoff arrangements.
- Tax, discount, promotion, specialized pricing, and role/access rules.
- Installation, onboarding, maintenance, warranty, support, and service-level terms.

These items are discussed during requirements gathering and become commitments only through an approved quotation and implementation scope.

## Not approved for publication as fact

- Unconfirmed public legal identity, address, phone, email, or privacy contact.
- Fixed pricing, packages, rollout dates, installation timelines, or response-time promises.
- Customers, deployments, testimonials, case studies, logos, ratings, certifications, or performance statistics without evidence and permission.
- Compatibility with an unnamed or unverified provider.
- Guaranteed SEO, GEO, AEO, ranking, traffic, or citation results.

## Visual sources

Official brand and kiosk artwork is stored in `assets/`. Product/payment/industry interface graphics in the website are original CSS compositions. Third-party references influenced design patterns only and are not cited or copied in the website.

The public kiosk experience is useful product context but is a limited presentation, not the complete source of truth for the commercial system. Product claims follow the later owner-confirmed scope above.
