## Exact recommended scene

- Opaque 4:3 landscape, matching first image's realistic daylight, neutral white balance, restrained blue/cyan and clean materials. Original Philippine specialty/accessories shop, not a copy of the reference store or an actual client claim.
- Medium-wide eye-level view from customer side, not a product close-up. Neatly arranged unbranded phone/accessory boxes and cases on shelves communicate retail immediately. Calm cream/light-wood surroundings with a clean glass-topped display/service counter; keep products organized rather than crowded.
- TWO fictional adults: adult male Filipino shopper LEFT in a plain neutral shirt; adult Filipina shop associate RIGHT behind counter in a plain light-blue blouse/navy trousers. Natural adult arm proportions, softly defined/slender arms and controlled sleeve bulk according to owner preference; realistic anatomy, not exaggerated thinness. No invented uniform badge or external brand.
- One small unbranded boxed accessory rests on the counter between them. Both naturally look toward the item during a quiet product-selection conversation, not at camera. Associate makes one restrained gesture toward the box; customer rests a hand nearby. Keep hands spatially separated, wrists plausible and no simultaneous cash/card/device exchange.
- Compact charcoal/silver landscape POS on the staff side near RIGHT counter end, deliberately aligned and grounded, not in customer circulation or covering the item. Screen faces the associate; camera sees rear/edge or only an oblique glimpse. Any screen glimpse is original-reference Ez Kyosk idle identity, not invented detailed sales/inventory UI. Do not rotate screen toward camera to advertise it or invent new rear branding.
- No standing kiosk in this second image: avoid repeating the first industry's kiosk/customer composition. Device is secondary to the retail environment and human interaction. This is a conceptual industry fit, not independent verification of a deployed retail installation.
- Keep both heads, important hands, product and POS support safely inside the frame. Lower-left image edge relatively quiet for the future HTML storefront badge. Soft background depth, enough detail for shop recognition at thumbnail size, no heavy blur that turns shelves into color smears.
- Original assets/ez-kyosk-lockup.png supplies any visible identity. Existing platform-pos-checkout-v1 supplies hardware family only; approved Hospitality v2 supplies lighting/material consistency, NOT the same people or cafe props.
- Do not add a cash handoff, QR/phone-payment action, card terminal focus, scanner action, receipt, queue screen, analytics, price promotion, sales/customer data or credit/invoicing/appointment claims. No payment-confirmation UI, third-party store names, headline, website icon badge, floating graphics, watermark or artificially blue-filtered scene.
