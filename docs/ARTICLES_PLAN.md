# Articles: proposal for owner brainstorming

Date: 17 November 2026, Asia/Manila. NOT implemented or approved for publication. Owner asked whether blog/news posts can improve SEO/AEO/GEO after deploying Home FAQ.

## Recommendation

Prefer an Articles or Guides resource library for Philippine business owners evaluating kiosk deployment, rather than a general news feed. Original, useful, evidence-backed content may expand relevant question coverage and internal links. Posting frequency, AI-generated volume or keyword repetition does not guarantee rankings or AI citations. Keep the current design-review preview unindexed; search launch remains a separate owner-approved gate.

Official guidance consulted 17 November 2026:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content

Google recommends original, helpful people-first content and existing SEO foundations for AI search features; scaled low-value generated pages may violate spam policies. Do not promise special schema, a required AI text file or guaranteed answer placement.
